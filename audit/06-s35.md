## 4. Pricing

### Rendered engine (`part3c.js:529–560`, `window.AFYAMB`)

- `530` `const PLAN={0:0,1:.03,6:.06,12:.09}` — member saving 3/6/9%, and 0 for buy-once ✅ (matches D4).
- `531` `const MS_D=.07,PDM=500` — Msingi: 7% under shelf, TSh 500 collection fee, rounded **down** to 100 and never above shelf (formula in `mk`, line 554).
- `534` `TADJ={E:0,C:0,L:.10,M:0}` — Luxury adds 10% back. This makes the Luxury monthly plan **above shelf** (−3% + 10% = +7%), which contradicts R3's condition that the member price be at or below shelf.
- `534` `PD=8500, PD_COST=1, PTS=.01, HUD=1.25`.
- `534` `PDBY` packing + delivery by size: S **7,000**, M **8,500**, L **10,000**. The comment there reads "box at landed cost by size (S 3,500 · M 5,000 · L 6,500) + delivery 3,500, charged at cost" ✅ (consistent with D4).
- `535` `const r500=v=>Math.ceil(v/500)*500` — **rounds UP**, while D4 says "rounded to the nearest 500". Part3b's version is `Math.round` (`part3b.js:877`).
- **Service fee: absent.** D4 has a "+ service fee" term; part3c has none. Services are priced separately at partner price × 1.25.
- `554` `mk`: `goods=S*(1-d)`; `box=r500(goods+pd)`; `pts=box*.01`; `gp=(box-pd-C)+pd*(1-PD_COST)-pts`.
- Afya's cost of goods is `COST={brand:.78,pharm:.72,own:.50}` (`part3c.js:11`), labelled "planning assumptions" (6–9).
- `model.js:17` `packing_delivery_in_price:8500` is a single flat value, while part3c varies it by size (7,000 / 8,500 / 10,000). **Part3c does not read `MODEL` at all** (0 references), which breaks K4.

### The 3/6/9 versus 13/16/20 conflict

- **part3b (dead for 3.5 but live through `window.AFYABOX` in 3.4):**
  - `part3b.js:531` comment says "member saving of 3%, 6% or 9%".
  - The code at `536` is `BASE=.08, PLD={1:0,3:.05,6:.08,12:.12}`, so the discount is `BASE+PLD` = **8 / 13 / 16 / 20%** (`884`).
  - Perks at `537–540`: "8% below shelf", "13%", "16%", "20% below shelf".
  - `part3b.js:1198` add-on note says "3% monthly, 6% on 6 months, 9% on 12 months" but computes `r500(...*(1-BASE))`, i.e. 8%.
  - `1111–1112` show "Member discount −8%" and "Plan discount 0/5/8/12%".
- **part3c:** uses 3/6/9 only.
- **Consequence:** the live page shows 3/6/9 in 3.5 while 3.4 computes with 8–20%. This is exactly the conflict the D4 note describes, now split across two engines.
