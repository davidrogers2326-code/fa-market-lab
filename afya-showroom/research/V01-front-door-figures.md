# Verification of the two health figures in section 0.1

**In plain language:** both figures on the home page are correct and now checked at source. Almost one adult in four in Tanzania has raised blood pressure (2023 national survey). Of every hundred adults with high blood pressure, about two have it under control; this comes from the 2012 national survey, the latest one that measured control. The page should say which survey each number comes from and the ages it covers.
