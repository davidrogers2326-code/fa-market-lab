# Exchange rates used across the research files

**In plain language:** every foreign price in the showroom is converted to Tanzanian shillings at one official rate, so the numbers agree with each other. The rate is the Bank of Tanzania's mean rate (halfway between its buying and selling rates) for trading date 10 November 2026. When a price is shown, the original currency, this rate and this date are shown beside it (brief B2).

| Currency | Buying | Selling | **Mean (used)** | Trading date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US dollar (USD) | 2,629.3564 | 2,655.6500 | **2,642.50** | 10 Nov 2026 |
| Chinese yuan (CNY) | 394.0350 | 397.9754 | **396.01** | 10 Nov 2026 |
| Indian rupee (INR) | 27.9905 | 28.2704 | **28.13** | 10 Nov 2026 |
| Kenyan shilling (KES) | 20.2258 | 20.4281 | **20.33** | 10 Nov 2026 |
| UAE dirham (AED) | 715.8997 | 723.0587 | **719.48** | 10 Nov 2026 |
| Euro (EUR) | 2,943.8275 | 2,973.2657 | **2,958.55** | 10 Nov 2026 |

Source: Bank of Tanzania, Exchange Rates, https://www.bot.go.tz/ExchangeRate/excRates. Opened 9 Nov 2026 (the page showed rates for trading date 10 Nov 2026).

Notes
- The old showroom used three different rates: 2,640 (no date), 2,644.05 (CurrencyBeacon, 2 Nov 2026) and KES × 20 (2 Nov 2026). From Phase 4 every section uses this table.
- Rates are refreshed each quarter, together with the shelf prices (brief D4).
