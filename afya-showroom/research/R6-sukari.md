# R6 — Sukari (living with diabetes, on tablets or on insulin)

Feeds section 3.5. Prices checked 10 Nov 2026. Data file: `data/boxes/sukari.json` (validated with node against `data/boxes/SCHEMA.md`: numeric `units` per item, `pack_units` per brand, exactly one `default` and one `afya_choice` per item, every visible string in English and Kiswahili). Old code key: `sugar` (`part3c.js`). Draft for the founder; the showroom code and the brief are unchanged.

## In plain language

- Sukari is for an adult with diabetes. It has two forms, modelled as stages: **on tablets** and **on insulin**. Each starts with a first box that brings the meter and the tools that last for years.
- **Strips follow the clinic's testing plan.** Formula: *strips per delivery = tests a week set by the clinic × weeks covered (4.3 a month, 13 a quarter) + 5 spare, rounded up to whole packs of 25.* Defaults: 4 tests a week on tablets (75 strips per 3-month box) and 16 a week on insulin (75 strips a month). One lancet per strip; on insulin one needle per injection (2 a day × 30 = 60).
- The strip brand always follows the meter: strips fit only their own meter. The Afya Choice is the MediSign meter (TSh 15,000) with MediSign strips (20,000 for 25), both on AfyaDepot.
- Tablets, insulin and ORS are kind "medicine": listed, supplied by the licensed partner pharmacy on prescription, **not priced** (B4).
- The B16 clinic line is on the page: the clinic decides how often to test, the targets, every medicine and dose, and the sick-day rules.
- **Price on the 12-month plan:** tablets 136,000 for the first 3-month box, then 87,000 every 3 months (TSh 397,000 a year, about 33,100 a month). Insulin 179,500 for the first month, then 104,500 a month (119,500 in months 4, 7 and 10, which add the sharps box, socks and toothpaste): TSh 1,374,000 a year, about 114,500 a month.
- **Does it pay its way?** On insulin, monthly delivery pays on every plan (12-month margin +13,367 a month). On tablets, a monthly box (shelf about 31,810) would lose 1,168 a month on the 12-month plan; **fix applied: the tablets box is delivered every 3 months** (12-month margin +8,997 per box). The free in-store check stays monthly.
- 121 of 239 brand rows are Tanzanian listings opened today; by value the default brands are 69–98% Tanzanian. Every core item has a sourced price, so `final` is **true**. Lancets, socks, pill boxes and clippers are Kenyan-only and need a Tanzanian quote.

## 1. Who it is for, and the guidance it is built from

**English.** For an adult living with type 2 diabetes (most) or type 1 diabetes, from diagnosis onwards, on tablets or insulin. In Tanzania's 2023 national STEPS survey (Mayige et al. 2026, opened 10 Nov 2026), **2.9%** of adults aged 18–69 had diabetes (6.5% at 46–69), and **72.4% of those found to have diabetes had never been diagnosed**; 17.2% were diagnosed and on medication and 10.3% diagnosed but not on medication. This is the same figure used in Chapter 1 (`research/chapter1-reference.html`). Many people therefore start late, with eye, foot and kidney damage under way, and need close home monitoring from the first month.

| Guidance | What the box does |
|---|---|
| WHO HEARTS-D (type 2 diabetes in primary care) and WHO HEARTS | Monthly in-store check (blood pressure, weight, the member's meter against the kiosk); refill and clinic reminders; tablets via the partner pharmacy |
| ADA Standards of Care 2025: self-monitoring tailored to treatment; 15 g glucose rule for a low (below 70 mg/dL, recheck after 15 min) | Strip formula from the clinic's plan; glucose powder sized to treatments (6 per quarter on tablets, 12 a month on insulin) |
| ADA 2025: yearly eye, kidney (eGFR, urine albumin) and dental checks; HbA1c every 3–6 months | Reminders; HbA1c and kidney tests as partner-lab add-ons; toothpaste and brush every 3 months |
| IWGDF 2023: inspect feet daily, emollient but not between toes, socks always, no barefoot walking, nails cut straight across | Foot cream, plain cotton socks every 3 months, toenail clipper, yearly monofilament screen in store |
| US FDA: home sharps in a rigid, lidded, puncture-proof box | 5 L sharps box (first box; on insulin every 3 months) |
| R1: glucose meters and strips are TMDA self-test IVDs (Class C); lancets and needles are TMDA devices; ORS is a pharmacy item | Afya sells meters, strips, lancets and needles as registered devices bought from a TMDA-registered local supplier; ORS, tablets and insulin go to the partner pharmacy |

**Tanzania.** The MoH's own diabetes guideline was not opened (no MoH host reachable from this session). The STEPS paper is Tanzanian (NIMR / MoH).

**Kiswahili.** See `who.sw` in the JSON (four paragraphs, same content).

### Clinic line (B16), shown on every Sukari page

- **EN:** How often you test your sugar, your targets, your tablets or insulin and their doses, and what to do on a sick day are decided by your clinic. Afya does not sell medicines: the licensed partner pharmacy supplies them only on the clinic’s prescription. A wound on the foot, a low that does not recover with glucose, or vomiting on insulin goes to the clinic the same day.
- **SW:** Mara ngapi upime sukari, malengo yako, vidonge au insulini na dozi zake, na la kufanya siku ya kuugua huamuliwa na kliniki yako. Afya haiuzi dawa: famasia mshirika yenye leseni huzitoa tu kwa maelekezo ya kliniki. Jeraha mguuni, sukari iliyoshuka isiyopanda baada ya glukosi, au kutapika ukiwa kwenye insulini, nenda kliniki siku hiyo hiyo.

## 7. Does the box pay its way?

- **Insulin, monthly: yes,** on every plan (12-month margins +13,367 and +17,553). Strips and needles (105,000 of the 114,759 monthly shelf) carry it.
- **Tablets, monthly: no.** One month of the same goods is about TSh 31,810: 1-month plan +812, 6-month -178, 12-month -1,168 a month with an S box and a delivery each month.
- **Fix applied: tablets box delivered every 3 months** (three months of goods, M box). It now pays on every plan (12-month +8,997 per box, 10.3%). Strips keep for many months sealed; the member still visits the store monthly for the free check. Recorded in the JSON as `stages[].delivery` = "every-3-months" (box-level `delivery` stays "monthly" for the insulin form; `delivery_note` explains both).
- Sensitivity: the 3-month tablets box turns negative if goods land above 81.3% of shelf (now 71.9%). Strip supplier quotes decide this: strips are 63% of the box value.
