# R6 — Figo (kidney care, before and on dialysis)

Feeds section 3.5. Prices checked 10 Nov 2026. Data file: `data/boxes/figo.json` (validated with node against `data/boxes/SCHEMA.md`). Old code key: `kidney` (`part3c.js`). Draft for the founder; the showroom code and the brief are unchanged.

## In plain language

- Figo has two forms, modelled as stages: **chronic kidney disease (CKD), not on dialysis** and **on haemodialysis**. Each starts with a first box of tools that last for years.
- **The fluid limit and the diet are decided by the renal clinic (B16).** The box supplies a measuring jug to make the clinic's number visible; it never states the number. Potassium salt substitutes, oats and nuts are deliberately left out (potassium and phosphorus are the clinic's call).
- **What Afya can sell, and what it cannot** (section 8): Afya sells only non-prescription goods (scale, monitor, thermometer, jug, log, pill box, sterile gauze, soap, emollient, sanitiser, sugar-free gum, alcohol-free rinse, lip balm). Dialysers, bloodlines, fistula needles, dialysis fluid, heparin, anaemia injections and catheter dressings come from the dialysis unit; tablets from the licensed partner pharmacy.
- **Delivery:** CKD every 3 months; dialysis every 2 months. Monthly boxes would lose money on the 12-month plan (CKD -3,750 a month; dialysis −353 in the regular months).
- **Price on the 12-month plan:** CKD 271,000 for the first box, then 78,500 every 3 months (TSh 506,500 a year, about 42,200 a month). Dialysis 329,500 for the first box, then 116,000 every 2 months (TSh 909,500 a year, about 75,800 a month).
- Margins on 12 months: CKD later box +1,746 (2.2%, thin), dialysis later box +7,087 (6.1%).
- 127 of 212 brand rows are Tanzanian listings opened today; 87–97% of default value is Tanzanian. `final` is **true**; gauze, jug, thermometers, spoons, pill boxes and water treatment are Kenyan-only.

## 1. Who it is for, and the guidance it is built from

**English.** For an adult with chronic kidney disease, most often caused by high blood pressure or diabetes, in two forms: (a) CKD not on dialysis, where the goal is to slow the loss of function; (b) haemodialysis two or three times a week, where the daily work is thirst, fluid gain, itch and the fistula. Community studies estimate CKD in 7–14% of Tanzanian adults; Tanzania has about 47 haemodialysis units with some 3,231 patients at USD 80–120 a session (Kidney360 2024, as cited in the earlier catalogue; **not re-opened**).

| Guidance | What the box does |
|---|---|
| KDIGO 2024 (CKD): BP control with standardised (incl. home) measurement; sodium < 2 g a day; avoid NSAIDs; medicine review at every visit; individual dietary advice | Monitor, log (incl. over-the-counter medicines), measuring spoons, salt-free seasoning, monthly medicine-list check in store; renal dietitian by referral |
| WHO sodium fact sheet (< 5 g salt) and WHO 2025 salt-substitute guideline (excludes kidney impairment; *exclusion seen in search summaries*) | Spoons and spices; **no potassium salt substitutes** |
| KDOQI 2019 vascular access: wash the access arm daily and before each session; no cuff, drip or blood draw on the access arm; feel the thrill daily | Mild soap for the fistula arm; monitor wording "arm without the fistula"; gauze for re-bleeding with pressure, never a tight bandage |
| 2023 systematic review: emollients first for uraemic xerosis and itch | Emollient 400 ml a month |
| BMC Nephrology 2025: sugar-free gum reduces thirst and dry mouth on haemodialysis | Sugar-free gum, alcohol-free rinse, lip balm |
| Cochrane 2015: household water treatment reduces diarrhoea (a cause of acute kidney injury) | Water treatment in the CKD box |
| R1: BP monitors and thermometers TMDA Class B; gauze a TMDA device; scales may need TMDA Class A notification | All sold as registered products bought from TMDA-registered suppliers |

**Tanzania.** No MoH CKD or dialysis guideline could be opened from this session. Units follow their own fluid and diet protocols; the box defers to them.

### Clinic line (B16), shown on every Figo page

- **EN:** Your fluid limit, your diet (salt, potassium, phosphate and protein), your blood pressure target, your medicines and doses, and how often you are tested are decided by your renal clinic or dialysis unit. Afya does not sell medicines or dialysis supplies: the licensed partner pharmacy supplies tablets on prescription, and the unit supplies what is used in each session. Fever of 38 °C or more, a fistula that stops buzzing, bleeding that does not stop, or sudden breathlessness needs the unit the same day.
- **SW:** Kiasi chako cha maji, lishe yako (chumvi, potasiamu, fosfeti na protini), lengo la presha, dawa na dozi zake, na mara ngapi upimwe huamuliwa na kliniki yako ya figo au kituo cha dialisisi. Afya haiuzi dawa wala vifaa vya dialisisi: famasia mshirika yenye leseni hutoa vidonge kwa maelekezo ya daktari, na kituo hutoa vinavyotumika kila kipindi. Homa ya nyuzi 38 au zaidi, fistula iliyoacha kutetemeka, damu isiyokoma, au kubanwa pumzi ghafla vinahitaji kituo siku hiyo hiyo.

## 7. Does the box pay its way?

- **CKD monthly: no.** About TSh 28,833 of goods a month loses 1,770 to 3,750 a month. **Fix applied: every 3 months** (M box). It now pays on every plan, but the later box is thin: +1,746 on 12 months; negative if goods land above 80% of shelf (now 78%).
- **Dialysis monthly: no on the 12-month plan.** A regular monthly box (soap, gauze, sanitiser, gum, rinse, emollient, spice; shelf 55,730) gives +3,112 / +1,627 / **−353** on the 1/6/12-month plans. **Fix applied: every 2 months** (two months of goods, M box): +7,087 on 12 months (6.1%). People on dialysis already travel to the unit 2–3 times a week, so fewer home deliveries also suit them.
- Box-level `delivery` is "every-3-months" (the CKD form); each stage carries its own `delivery` ("every-3-months" or "every-2-months") and `delivery_note` explains both.
