## 1. Who it is for, and the guidance it is built from

**English.**

Kijana is the box for a teenage boy aged 12 to 18. It handles puberty without awkward conversations: soap and an antiperspirant for the body odour that starts with puberty, a gentle face wash for spots, fluoride toothpaste and a fresh brush, body lotion, a pocket sanitiser and a mosquito repellent. From about 15 it adds razors and shaving foam for the first shave. The first box brings what sport needs: a water bottle, plasters, an instant cold pack for sprains and a nail clipper, plus a treated net.

**Why.** Puberty switches on the sweat glands and oil glands; body odour and acne are the commonest reasons teenage boys are teased, and both respond to simple daily care. WHO advises at least an hour of moderate-to-vigorous activity a day for adolescents, and most do less; boys who play football in Dar heat need water and basic first aid. WHO recommends twice-daily brushing with a 1,000–1,500 ppm fluoride toothpaste; teenagers drink more sugary sodas than younger children. Insecticide-treated nets are one of WHO’s two core malaria protections. Razor bumps are common in boys with tightly curled hair, and the shaving items follow dermatology advice on shaving with the grain, on softened skin, with a fresh blade.

**Medicines.** The only medicine in the list is benzoyl peroxide 2.5% gel for acne that a wash alone does not settle. It is supplied by the partner pharmacy on its advice and is not priced in the box.

**When to see a clinic.** Acne that scars or has not improved after two months, a sports injury with severe pain, a misshapen joint or inability to stand, and any lump, pain or swelling in the testicles, which needs a doctor the same day.

**Kiswahili.**

Kijana ni sanduku la mvulana balehe wa miaka 12 hadi 18. Linashughulikia balehe bila mazungumzo ya aibu: sabuni na roll-on ya kuzuia jasho kwa harufu ya mwili inayoanza na balehe, sabuni laini ya uso kwa chunusi, dawa ya meno yenye floraidi na mswaki mpya, losheni ya mwili, kitakasa mikono cha mfukoni na dawa ya kufukuza mbu. **Kwa nini.** Balehe huwasha tezi za jasho na za mafuta; harufu ya mwili na chunusi ndizo sababu za kawaida zaidi za wavulana balehe kudhihakiwa, na zote hupungua kwa utunzaji rahisi wa kila siku. **Dawa.** Dawa pekee kwenye orodha ni jeli ya benzoyl peroxide 2.5% kwa chunusi ambazo sabuni laini pekee haizipunguzi. Hutolewa na famasia mshirika kwa ushauri wake na haina bei ndani ya sanduku.

## 7. Does it pay its way?

**Check (D4):** price − goods at landed cost − packing − delivery (3,500) − 1% points ≥ 0. Landed cost uses R3's planning shares (brand 78%, pharm 72%, own 50%).

**Monthly, as in the code (S box, packing 3,500 + delivery 3,500):**

| Box | Monthly shelf | 1-month | 6-month | 12-month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Every two months · ages 12–14 | 23,618 | -2,652 | -3,642 | -4,137 |
| Every two months · ages 15–18, first shave | 26,129 | -2,136 | -3,126 | -3,621 |

Plainly: **no.** Monthly delivery loses money on every plan.

**Fix applied: every two months.** Margin per box:

| Box | Size | Delivered on its own: 1 / 6 / 12-month | Riding inside another Afya box (no delivery; S inner pack): 1 / 6 / 12-month |
|---|---|---|---|
| First box · ages 12–14 | M | 14,979 / 12,504 / 10,029 | 19,979 / 17,504 / 15,029 |
| Every two months · ages 12–14 | S | 1,696 / 211 / **-1,274** | 5,196 / 3,711 / 2,226 |
| Every two months · ages 15–18, first shave | S | 2,233 / 748 / **-737** | 5,733 / 4,248 / 2,763 |

**Result.** One box every two months, packed with two months of goods. Delivered on its own it pays its way on every plan except: Every two months · ages 12–14 on the 12-month plan; Every two months · ages 15–18, first shave on the 12-month plan. Those boxes ride inside another Afya box delivered to the same household (one delivery, no extra delivery cost) or are collected in store. Monthly delivery is not offered: a one-month basket cannot carry packing and delivery.

Over a year of boxes delivered on their own (first box once, then the boxes a year shown in section 6), the margin is 38,553 (1-month), 18,258 (6-month), -2,037 (12-month); with the failing boxes riding, every box is positive. Margins stay thin (1–7% of price on recurring boxes); they turn negative if goods land above about 80–82% of shelf instead of 78%.
