## 1. Who it is for, and the guidance it is built from

**English.**

Afya Shule is the school-year box for a child aged 4 to 12 in Dar es Salaam, from the first year of pre-primary school to the end of primary school. It arrives every two months, timed to the school calendar: a big box in January for the start of the year, term boxes in March, July and September, and holiday boxes in May and November that carry play and travel items for the June and December holidays.

**Why these items.** The common illnesses of school-age children in Tanzania are the ones that clean hands, clean teeth, a net and periodic deworming prevent. WHO recommends periodic deworming of all school-age children where soil-transmitted worms are common, and Tanzania gives it free in school campaigns; the box reminds parents before each holiday and the partner pharmacy covers a missed round. WHO advises twice-daily brushing with a 1,000–1,500 ppm fluoride toothpaste, which matters most once the first permanent molars come through at about six. Insecticide-treated nets are one of WHO’s two core malaria protections, and many nets sold in shops are untreated. Safe water, sanitation and handwashing with soap prevent diarrhoea (WHO). Tanzania’s school health and school WASH guidelines set the hygiene the morning inspection checks: clean body, short hair, short nails.

**Learning and play.** Exercise books and pencils run out every few weeks, so the term boxes bring them. WHO asks for about an hour of activity a day for children; in the holidays a ball replaces the school timetable.

**What it does not do.** Medicines in the box list (deworming tablet, paracetamol syrup, ORS) are supplied only by the licensed partner pharmacy, on its advice, and are not priced in the box. Food is not assigned: porridge flour and snacks are chosen by the parent from the nutrition shelf.

**Kiswahili.**

Afya Shule ni sanduku la mwaka wa shule kwa mtoto wa miaka 4 hadi 12 jijini Dar es Salaam, kuanzia mwaka wa kwanza wa elimu ya awali hadi mwisho wa shule ya msingi. Linakuja kila baada ya miezi miwili, likifuata kalenda ya shule: sanduku kubwa Januari kwa mwanzo wa mwaka, masanduku ya muhula Machi, Julai na Septemba, na masanduku ya likizo Mei na Novemba yenye vitu vya kuchezea na vya safari kwa likizo za Juni na Desemba.

**Kwa nini vitu hivi.** Magonjwa ya kawaida ya watoto wa umri wa shule Tanzania ni yale yanayozuiwa na mikono safi, meno safi, chandarua na dawa za minyoo kwa vipindi. WHO inapendekeza dawa za minyoo kwa vipindi kwa watoto wote wa umri wa shule pale minyoo ya udongoni ilipo kwa wingi, na Tanzania huzitoa bure katika kampeni za shule; sanduku huwakumbusha wazazi kabla ya kila likizo na famasia mshirika hushughulikia awamu iliyokosekana. WHO inashauri kupiga mswaki mara mbili kwa siku kwa dawa ya meno yenye floraidi ya ppm 1,000–1,500, jambo muhimu zaidi magego ya kwanza ya kudumu yanapoota karibu na miaka sita. Vyandarua vyenye dawa ni mojawapo ya kinga kuu mbili za malaria za WHO, na vyandarua vingi vinavyouzwa madukani havina dawa. Maji salama, vyoo na kunawa mikono kwa sabuni huzuia kuhara (WHO). Miongozo ya Tanzania ya huduma za afya shuleni na ya maji, usafi wa mazingira na usafi shuleni inaweka usafi unaokaguliwa asubuhi: mwili safi, nywele fupi, kucha fupi.

**Kujifunza na kucheza.** Madaftari na penseli huisha kila baada ya wiki chache, hivyo masanduku ya muhula huyaleta. WHO inataka watoto wafanye shughuli za mwili takriban saa moja kwa siku; likizoni mpira huchukua nafasi ya ratiba ya shule.

**Lisichofanya.** Dawa zilizo kwenye orodha ya sanduku (kidonge cha minyoo, sharubati ya paracetamol, ORS) hutolewa na famasia mshirika yenye leseni tu, kwa ushauri wake, na hazina bei ndani ya sanduku. Chakula hakipangwi: unga wa uji na vitafunio huchaguliwa na mzazi kutoka rafu ya lishe.

### Clinic line, shown on every Afya Shule page

- **EN:** Any medicine for your child, its dose for the child’s weight, and whether a deworming dose is needed outside the school campaign are decided by your clinic or the partner pharmacist. A fever in Tanzania needs a malaria test the same day; a child who cannot drink, is very drowsy or has blood in the stool goes to a clinic at once.
- **SW:** Dawa yoyote kwa mtoto wako, dozi yake kulingana na uzito wa mtoto, na kama dozi ya minyoo inahitajika nje ya kampeni ya shule huamuliwa na kliniki yako au mfamasia mshirika. ## 7. Does it pay its way?

**Check (D4):** price − goods at landed cost − packing − delivery (3,500) − 1% points ≥ 0. Landed cost uses R3's planning shares (brand 78%, pharm 72%, own 50%).

**Monthly, as in the code (S box, packing 3,500 + delivery 3,500):**

| Box | Monthly shelf | 1-month | 6-month | 12-month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| School-term box (March, July, September) | 21,493 | -1,752 | -2,742 | -3,237 |
| Holiday box (May and November) | 23,710 | -2,113 | -2,608 | -3,598 |

Plainly: **no.** Monthly delivery loses money on every plan.

**Fix applied: every two months.** Margin per box:

| Box | Size | Delivered on its own: 1 / 6 / 12-month | Riding inside another Afya box (no delivery; S inner pack): 1 / 6 / 12-month |
|---|---|---|---|
| January · first box of the school year | M | 15,309 / 13,329 / 10,854 | 20,309 / 18,329 / 15,854 |
| School-term box (March, July, September) | S | 3,001 / 2,011 / 526 | 6,501 / 5,511 / 4,026 |
| Holiday box (May and November) | S | 2,775 / 1,290 / **-195** | 6,275 / 4,790 / 3,305 |

**Result.** One box every two months, packed with two months of goods. Delivered on its own it pays its way on every plan except: Holiday box (May and November) on the 12-month plan. Those boxes ride inside another Afya box delivered to the same household (one delivery, no extra delivery cost) or are collected in store. Monthly delivery is not offered: a one-month basket cannot carry packing and delivery.

Over a school year of six boxes delivered on their own (the January box, three term boxes and two holiday boxes), the margin is 29,862 (1-month), 21,942 (6-month), 12,042 (12-month); with the failing boxes riding, every box is positive. Margins stay thin on recurring boxes (5.0% / 2.9% of price on the 6-month plan delivered alone). Landed-cost share of shelf at which the margin reaches 0, now about 72% / 75%: 6-month alone 77% / 78%; 12-month riding 82% / 82%. Supplier quotes decide whether these hold.
