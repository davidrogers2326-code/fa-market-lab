# R6 — Afya Glow (women aged 20–59)

Feeds section 3.5 (package page). Prices checked 10 Nov 2026. Data file: `data/boxes/afya-glow.json` (built from this research; `final: true`, reasons in section 6). Period care is the separate Afya Siku box (B16).

## In plain language

- **Who it is for.** Women aged 20–59 in Dar es Salaam. The box holds their everyday skin, hair and personal care for a hot, humid city with strong sun every day.
- **What is in it (13 items, one box a month):** gentle face cleanser, face moisturiser, daily broad-spectrum sunscreen, body lotion, a mild beauty bar, an antiperspirant, shampoo and leave-in conditioner for natural hair, fluoride toothpaste, a toothbrush (one every 3 months), dental floss, lip balm, and one spare pack of pads.
- **What it never holds:** skin lighteners, "whitening" soaps or lotions, and steroid creams for the face. WHO warns that lighteners often contain mercury and other hazardous chemicals such as hydroquinone. Tanzania bans these products (TBS list, seen only in a search result). The safe route to an even tone is daily sunscreen and gentle care, plus a partner skin consultation for dark patches or acne.
- **Shelf value:** TSh 47,973 a month at the default brands. 58% of that value is from Tanzanian listings checked on 10 Nov 2026 (AfyaDepot, Shoppers). The rest is Kenyan prices, converted at KES 20.33 and not re-checked.
- **Member price (Regular):** TSh 46,500 (1-month), 45,000 (6-month Gold), 43,500 (12-month Platinum). Packing and delivery are included (D4).
- **Taste of luxury** (priced as its own line): Premium adds Bio-Oil 60 ml (TSh 18,200, Shoppers). Luxury adds a Bath & Body Works shower gel (TSh 32,528, Kenyan price).
- **Service:** one partner skin consultation a year, **about TSh 35,000. This is an EST (planning, no quote).** It is charged only in the month it is used.
- **The problem:** delivered monthly, the Regular box **does not pay its way** on the 6- and 12-month plans. It loses TSh 1,369 and 2,854 a month, because a TSh 48k basket cannot carry TSh 8,500 of packing and delivery at a 9% saving. **Proposed fix:** deliver two months of goods every two months. All plans then make TSh 1,643–4,366 a month. Members who want a box every month can collect it in store, or take Premium or Luxury, whose luxury line covers the delivery.
- **Not well sourced:** no Tanzanian sunscreen in the price range was found. All four sunscreen rows are Kenyan. The cleanser, leave-in, lip balm and pad defaults are Kenyan too.

## 1. Who it is for, and the guidance it is built from

**Who.** Women from 20 to 59 who live in Dar es Salaam. The city sits about 7° south of the equator, with heat and humidity all year. The needs this creates are steady and predictable:
- sweat and oil on the face and body;
- natural hair that dries and breaks;
- dark marks that the sun makes darker.

The box gives a complete, plain routine and nothing more:
- **Face:** cleanse, moisturise, protect from the sun.
- **Body:** a mild bar and lotion, plus an antiperspirant.
- **Hair:** a weekly wash and a conditioner.
- **Mouth:** fluoride toothpaste, a toothbrush and floss.
- **Lips:** a balm.
- **Period:** one spare pack of pads.

Older members (50–59) use the same routine. Post-menopausal skin is drier, so a richer lotion such as Dove or Bodidoc is a free swap at the same price range.

**Skin lightening.** Lightening products are a well-documented health risk.
- **What WHO says.** Skin-lightening products reduce the skin's melanin, often with toxic mercury compounds. Mercury damages the kidneys and nervous system and causes skin problems. Exposure of the fetus is a particular danger. WHO names hydroquinone among the other hazardous chemicals in these products.
- **What the law says.** The Minamata Convention (Article 4) bars the manufacture, import and export of cosmetics with more than 1 ppm of mercury.
- **What Tanzania does.** Tanzania moved cosmetics regulation to the Tanzania Bureau of Standards in 2019. TBS publishes a list of banned products that names hydroquinone creams and mercury soaps. This is **from search results only**: the TBS PDF and the news pages could not be opened from this session.
- **What Afya does.** Afya Basics never sells or recommends a lightener. The store offers a free label check of any cream a member brings in. The partner skin consultation is the route for melasma or acne.
- **Not used.** The earlier showroom code quoted a Kinondoni survey (Francis et al. 2025, "54.5% of women use lightening products"). The article could not be opened (pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov refused) and a search did not find it, so that figure is **not used** anywhere.

## 2. What changed from the earlier code (`part3c.js` `D.PKG.glow`, "Women's skin & care")

- **Age.** The age range is now 20–59 (B16). The earlier code said 18–45.
- **Tiers.** Essential/Comfort/Luxury brand ladders became one list of 3–5 choices per item, in roughly the same price range. Premium and Luxury differ only by one luxury line, priced outside the saving (D4).
- **Removed as filler or against guidance:**
  - **even-tone serum** (niacinamide or vitamin C, TSh 10–22k a month). No guideline that could be opened supports it. Dark patches go to the skin consultation instead. The only vitamin C serum under TSh 50,000 on AfyaDepot (Disaar, 40,000) sits at a "whitening" web address.
  - **intimate wash.** The NHS advice the earlier code itself cited is plain water and mild soap. This is the same decision as in Mama na Mtoto.
  - **razors, cotton buds and hand cream.** These are personal choices, not health needs. They are available as add-ons.
  - **the monthly gift** (bonnet, mist, Bio-Oil), which is replaced by the tier luxury line.
- **Added:** one spare pack of pads. The toothbrush is now split from the toothpaste, and floss is kept.
- **Quantities corrected:**
  - toothpaste 100 → 60 g a month;
  - lip balm 4 g a month instead of "a tin every 3 months";
  - body wash 250 g or ml instead of 3 bars.
- **Prices:**
  - Every Kenyan row is recomputed from its KES original at 20.33. The code used ×20.
  - AfyaDepot and Shoppers prices were re-read on 10 Nov 2026. Colgate Maximum Protection 140 g is now 5,900 at Shoppers; the code had 3,950 from superstore.co.tz.
  - The superstore.co.tz and findandgetgroup.com rows (refused hosts) were dropped where a Tanzanian alternative existed.
- **Afya Choice rule.** It is the lowest monthly cost among suitable rows whose pack lasts five months or less. A Tanzanian row is preferred when it is within 35% of the cheapest. The default brand for the headline price is the Afya Choice.
  - Suitability excludes Whitedent until its fluoride level is confirmed.
  - Disaar "SPF 90" sunscreens (AfyaDepot, TSh 15,000/80 g and 35,000/150 ml) are left out of the choices. The listings state no UVA protection, EU labelling stops at "50+", so an SPF 90 label is not a reliable claim, and the same brand sells a serum at a "whitening" web address.

## 6. Price check (D4)

- **Formula.** Shelf value = Σ default-brand price × units ÷ pack units, over the 13 goods. Medicines: none. The luxury line is excluded.
- **Member price.** Shelf × (1 − 3% / 6% / 9%), rounded to the nearest 500 and never above shelf. Packing M (5,000) and delivery (3,500) are included.
- **Margin.** Price − goods at landed cost (78% of shelf, the R3 planning share for branded goods, not a supplier quote) − packing − delivery − 1% points.

**Shelf value: TSh 47,973 a month** (TZ 27,656 = 58%; KE 20,318 = 42%; EST 0).

| Delivery option | 1-month (−3%) | 6-month Gold (−6%) | 12-month Platinum (−9%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly, delivered (M box), as planned | 46,500 · margin **+116** (0.2%) | 45,000 · margin **−1,369** (−3.0%) | 43,500 · margin **−2,854** (−6.6%) |
| Monthly, delivered in an S box (if it fits) | 46,500 · +1,616 | 45,000 · +131 | 43,500 · −1,354 |

**Verdict: the Regular box does not pay its way monthly.** Under D4, a monthly-delivered M box needs a shelf value of about **TSh 70,300** before the 12-month plan breaks even (8,500 ÷ (0.91 − 0.78 − 0.0091)). Glow's honest basket is TSh 48k, because skin, hair and oral care are cheap per month. The box was not padded with filler to reach 70k.

**Proposed fix (for the founder):**
1. **Regular is delivered every two months, with two months of goods** (about TSh 96k shelf per box). The member still pays monthly: 46,500 / 45,000 / 43,750 a month. Every plan is then positive. Bulky items (the lotion, the shampoo) already last two months or more, so the box size stays M. This is the D4 remedy ("delivered less often"), the same one used for the pregnancy boxes.
2. A member who wants a box every month can **collect it in store** at the same price: all plans are positive (+646 to +3,616).
3. **Premium and Luxury can be delivered monthly**: their luxury line covers the delivery (+968 and +3,977 on 12 months).
4. Do not move Glow to an S box to rescue monthly delivery. Month 1 ships full bottles (725 ml lotion, 500 ml shampoo, 500 ml leave-in), and even then the 12-month plan still loses 1,354.

The JSON keeps `delivery: "monthly"` as instructed. The fix is stated in `open` and needs the founder's decision. If it is approved, set `delivery` to a two-monthly value; SCHEMA.md would then need that value added.
