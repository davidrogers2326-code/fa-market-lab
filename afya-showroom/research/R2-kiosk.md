# R2 — Afya Check Station (feeds 3.3 and 4.1)

Checked 9 November 2026. Exchange rate used throughout: **US$1 = TSh 2,642.50**, Bank of Tanzania mean rate for trading date 10 Nov 2026 (`research/fx.md`). Every TSh figure below gives the US$ original, this rate and this date.

All supplier prices below are **listing prices** from public marketplace pages. None of them is a quote. No supplier has been contacted.

---

## In plain language

1. We recommend a **basic wellness kiosk**: one standing unit that measures height, weight, BMI, body composition, blood pressure, blood oxygen (SpO2) and temperature, and prints the results. It has no blood tests, no urine test and no vision test.
2. Several Chinese makers list kiosks like this. The cheapest basic units list at **US$1,155–2,700** (Leka HW-900G, Dingheng DHM-900C and DHM-T90). Units with a hospital-style finish list at **US$4,490–4,990** (Shanghe; this is the unit the showroom quotes today).
3. The brief's figure of US$3,267–3,630 does appear, but only in a search snippet for a Sonka E600A listing. I could not open that page.
4. Landed in Dar es Salaam, a budget unit costs about **TSh 7.6–9.8M** including 18% VAT, or **TSh 6.6–8.6M** if Afya can reclaim the VAT. A Shanghe-class unit costs about **TSh 19.1–22.2M**. Freight and clearing are estimates.
5. The brief's "≈ TSh 9–10M" is right only for a budget unit. For a US$3,267–3,630 unit, TSh 9–10M is roughly the factory price alone. Landed, that unit costs **TSh 14.7–17.9M**.
6. Import duty is **0%** if the kiosk is entered as a medical instrument (HS 9018.19.00, EAC tariff). Import VAT is **18%** and the railway levy is **2%**. HS 9018 is not on the TBS pre-shipment inspection (PVoC) list, so **no PVoC certificate** should be needed. The kiosk will probably need a TMDA import permit instead (R1).
7. Running the kiosk for a year is estimated at **TSh 1.9–4.8M**. That covers one repair, a spare cuff, paper, calibration checks, cleaning supplies and data. All of these are estimates.
8. Changes to the showroom page: **"15 measurements" becomes 7.** Vision screening and the finger-prick add-ons come off, as does "Add-ons later: ECG · glucose · cholesterol · urine". The price changes from US$4,990 + 20% to the landed table below. The timing changes from "about 4 minutes" to a **50-second quick check plus about 2–3 minutes for the full check** (estimate).
9. I found no supplier or Tanzanian distributor that leases or rents these kiosks. Buy or lease stays a founder decision; the factors are set out below.
10. Still unknown: written quotes, real certificates, warranty terms in writing, a freight quote and a clearing quote. Also unknown: whether TMDA requires registration, and whether any supplier can supply a Kiswahili screen and SMS results.

Add a **spare kit bought with the kiosk** (one cuff, one printer module, ten paper rolls): about US$150–300 (estimate) → TSh 396,375–792,750. It goes in the capital line, not running costs. For section 4.1, carry **"Kiosk maintenance ≈ TSh 2–5M a year (estimate)"** until quotes arrive.

**Calibration needs**
- **Blood-pressure cuff and module.**
  - Ask each supplier for the BP module's clinical-validation certificate (ISO 81060-2 or an ESH/AAMI protocol), its stated cuff-pressure accuracy and its cuff size range.
  - Check it once a year against a calibrated reference manometer (static pressure test), and after any repair.
  - Replace the cuff when it is worn.
  - A fixed "arm-cylinder" cuff (as on Shanghe's SH-T16) cannot be swapped for a large-arm cuff, so ask what arm sizes it fits.
- **Scale.**
  - Zero it daily.
  - Check it monthly with known test weights (for example 20 kg and 50 kg) and once a year by an external lab.
  - Whether the Weights and Measures Agency must verify a scale that is not used for trade is unconfirmed; `www.wma.go.tz` was blocked.
- **Height sensor:** check monthly against a fixed reference rod.
- **SpO2 and temperature:** check function against a separate approved device each month. These modules are normally replaced rather than calibrated (confirm with the supplier).
- **Body composition (bioimpedance):** a screen warning is needed. People with pacemakers or other implanted electronic devices, and pregnant women, should skip the body-composition step. This matters for Mama na Mtoto members. The exact wording should come from the supplier's manual (open item).

## 6. Recommended specification (for the founder to approve)

This is a recommendation only. Nothing is ordered until the founder approves and three written quotes arrive.

- **Type:** a single standing self-service wellness kiosk, basic tier, with a floor footprint of about 0.3–0.5 m² (K1 is 43 × 61 cm; K3 is 165 × 72 × 58 cm).
- **Measurements:**
  - ultrasonic height;
  - weight from load cells, 0.1 kg resolution;
  - BMI;
  - 8-electrode bioimpedance body composition;
  - upper-arm cuff blood pressure with pulse, using a clinically validated module;
  - fingertip SpO2;
  - non-contact infrared temperature.
- **Not included:** blood or urine modules, ECG, vision and coin operation.
- **Screen and UI:** 15–22" touchscreen. The supplier loads Afya's Kiswahili and English screen text (K2 and K3 state "All languages can be customized"; K1 and K4 to confirm).
- **Results:** thermal printout plus a QR code. SMS to come from Afya's system through the kiosk's WiFi or LAN link; no listing offers SMS (R5).
- **Power:** AC 100–240 V 50/60 Hz (K1 and K2 state this), plus a small UPS.
- **Documents to require with each quote:**
  - CE certificate with notified-body number for the BP and SpO2 functions;
  - the factory's ISO 13485 certificate;
  - BP clinical-validation report;
  - scale accuracy class (for example OIML);
  - user manual with contraindications;
  - warranty of at least 12 months in writing;
  - spare-parts price list and lead time;
  - remote-support terms.
- **Shortlist for written quotes:**
  - K2 Dingheng DHM-900C (or K3 DHM-T90);
  - K1 Leka HW-900G;
  - K4 Shanghe (the current showroom unit), as the higher-finish benchmark;
  - K5 Sonka E600A as an alternate, if its page can be opened.
- **Budget to carry in 3.3 and 6.3 until quotes arrive:** **TSh 10M landed (estimate)** for one budget-class unit (column A high, VAT paid), with the components listed as in section 2b. Carry **TSh 2–5M a year** maintenance (estimate) in 4.1.
