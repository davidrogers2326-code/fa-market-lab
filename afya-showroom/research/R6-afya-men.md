# R6 — Afya Men (men aged 20–59)

Feeds section 3.5 (package page, second in the R6 order). Prices checked 10 Nov 2026. Data file: `data/boxes/afya-men.json` (`final: true`: every core item has a sourced shelf price; see section 6 for the margin problem, which is a separate issue).

## In plain language

- **Who:** one man aged 20–59 in Dar es Salaam. **What:** a month of everyday grooming and health goods, plus a free blood-pressure check (the in-store Afya Check) every month.
- **The health reason:** almost one Tanzanian adult in four (23.1%) had raised blood pressure in the 2023 national survey, rising to 46.4% at ages 46–69. Most feel nothing. The box gives a man a monthly reason to walk past the Afya Check.
- **Ten items, no filler:** disposable razors, shaving foam, an antiperspirant roll-on, bath soap, face wash, a face sunscreen, body lotion, fluoride toothpaste, a toothbrush (every third month) and dental floss. No medicines.
- **Shelf value TSh 40,061 a month** on the Afya Choice brands. Member price (packing and delivery included, D4): **TSh 39,000** (1-month plan), **37,500** (6-month Gold), **36,500** (12-month Platinum).
- **Taste of luxury** (its own line, at shelf price): Premium adds a Nivea after-shave balm (TSh 17,000, Tanzanian price). Luxury adds a Bath & Body Works men's body spray (TSh 45,742, Kenyan price seen only in a search summary).
- **Sources:** 45 brand rows; 32 re-checked today on Tanzanian sites (22 Shoppers, 10 AfyaDepot), 13 Kenyan prices not re-checked. 70% of the default box value is from Tanzanian listings. Disposable razors have no Tanzanian online listing.
- **The problem, stated plainly:** at about TSh 40,000 the box **does not pay its way with monthly home delivery** on the 6- and 12-month plans (it loses TSh 1,123 and 2,113 a month; the 1-month plan just clears, +362). Packing and delivery (TSh 7,000) are too large a share of a small basket.
- **Proposed fix (founder decision):** (1) the default is **collect in store at the monthly Afya Check**, which pays on every plan (+1,387 to +3,862) and puts the man in front of the BP kiosk; (2) home delivery is offered **every two months** (two months' goods in one box, +1,275 to +5,730); (3) monthly home delivery on the 6- and 12-month plans only with a basket of at least **TSh 45,500 / 56,900**. A Nivea SPF 50 sunscreen as the default (instead of the doubtful Disaar SPF 90) would raise the box to TSh 56,957 and make monthly delivery just pass (+54 on 12 months).

## 1. Who it is for, and the guidance it is built from

**Who.** Men aged 20 to 59 in Dar es Salaam: office workers, traders, drivers and outdoor workers. They live in heat and humidity all year, most shave several times a week, and in their working years they rarely see a clinic when they feel well. The box covers one man's monthly grooming and oral care and turns the monthly visit into a blood-pressure screen.

**Why blood pressure is the centre.** In the 2023 Tanzania STEPS survey (adults 18–69), raised blood pressure (≥140/90 or on medicine) was 23.1%; 12.6% at ages 18–30 and 46.4% at 46–69; 36.4% in overweight or obese adults against 19.2% at normal weight (Mayige et al. 2026, abstract opened 10 Nov). In the 2012 survey, the last to measure the cascade, 17.6% of adults with hypertension were aware, 8.3% treated and 2.2% controlled (WHO country profile, checked 9 Nov). WHO's fact sheet: most people with hypertension feel no symptoms; "the only way to detect hypertension is to have a health professional measure blood pressure"; the diagnosis needs readings ≥140/90 on two different days (opened 10 Nov). The STEPS abstract does not give a men/women split, so the box does not claim one.

**When to see a clinic** (in the JSON `who` text): a reading of 140/90 or more at the Afya Check (screening only: confirmed by a clinic on a second day); about 180/120 with symptoms (immediately, WHO); razor bumps that become painful, pus-filled or leave spreading dark marks; bleeding or swollen gums (WHO names these as signs of gum disease); any new or changing spot on the skin. The last two referral rules are Afya's service design, not quoted guidance.

**Clinic line (in the JSON):** "The blood-pressure reading at the Afya Check is a screening, not a diagnosis. Whether you have high blood pressure, how often to check it and any medicine are decided by your clinic; a reading of 180/120 or more with chest pain, severe headache, breathlessness or blurred vision needs care immediately."

## 2. What changed from the earlier showroom box (`part3c.js` `D.PKG.man`, "Men's care")

- **Added:** dental floss (NHS, cleaning between teeth); bath soap bars (the old box used shower gel at 250 ml a month; a bar is the cheaper effective wash in Dar).
- **Changed:** body wash → bar soap; "body and face moisturiser" → body lotion, with the face covered by the sunscreen; sunscreen moved from Comfort/Luxury into the core (WHO UV guidance); toothbrush charged at one third a month.
- **Prices.** Kenyan rows are recomputed from their KES originals at 20.33 (the old code used ×20). 32 rows are Tanzanian listings re-opened today. The old Shoppers rows used here (Mach3 cartridges, Gillette foam and gel, Vaseline lotion, Sensodyne, Oral-B, Colgate brush) were all re-opened and unchanged.
- **Afya Choice** is the lowest-cost suitable row a month with a real listing; "suitable" excludes a roll-on not stated to be an antiperspirant (Brut), a toothpaste whose fluoride is not stated (Whitedent), and the system cartridges (they need a handle). The Afya Choice is the default for the headline price.
- **Services.** The old box listed five services. Only the free in-store Afya Check is kept (`bp-check`, price 0). The yearly doctor check-up, skin consultation and dental check are referrals, not box services, until R7 prices them with a partner.

## 6. Price check (D4)

- **Formula.** Shelf value = Σ default-brand price × units ÷ pack units (10 goods items; no medicines; the luxury line excluded).
- **Member price.** Shelf × (1 − 3% / 6% / 9%), nearest 500, never above shelf. Packing (S 3,500) and delivery (3,500) are inside the price.
- **Check.** Price − goods at landed cost (78% of shelf, R3 planning share for branded goods, `part3c.js` `COST.brand`; not a supplier quote) − packing − delivery − 1% points ≥ 0.

**The box does not pay its way with monthly home delivery on the 6- and 12-month plans.** The smallest monthly basket that passes with home delivery is TSh 37,900 (1-month), 45,500 (6-month) and 56,900 (12-month).

**Proposed fix (founder decision, D4 "delivered less often or collected in store"):**
1. **Default: collect in store, monthly, at the Afya Check.** Passes on every plan and is the health design of the box: the man is measured each time he collects.
2. **Home delivery every two months** (one M box carrying two months' goods). Passes on every plan. The JSON keeps `delivery: "monthly"` as instructed; if the founder approves, the page states "collect monthly in store, or delivered every two months".
3. **Monthly home delivery** only on the 1-month plan, or on 6/12-month plans when the member's basket reaches TSh 45,500 / 56,900 (e.g. with a higher-range sunscreen or the luxury line added; the luxury line's own margin is not counted here).
- Sensitivity: replacing the Disaar sunscreen with Nivea Sun Shine Control SPF 50 (KE) as default raises the shelf to TSh 56,957 and gives 55,000 / 53,500 / 52,000 with margins +3,024 / +1,539 / +54: monthly home delivery would just pass, but the 12-month margin would be close to zero and the line would rest on a Kenyan price.
- If landed cost turns out above 78%, the 12-month in-store margin (1,387 = 3.5% of shelf) disappears at about 81.5%.
