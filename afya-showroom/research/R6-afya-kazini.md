# R6 — Afya Kazini (the office health and first-aid box)

Feeds section 3.5 (package page) and the Afya Kazini panel in section 0/5.2. Prices checked 10 Nov 2026. Data file: `data/boxes/afya-kazini.json` (built from this research; `final: false`, reasons in section 7). Decision D-3: Afya Kazini is the office box. The small-business trade kits (shops, food stalls, salons, workshops) are the separate package Afya Biashara and are not covered here.

## In plain language

- **What it is.** A monthly box that keeps an office's first-aid box stocked, its basins supplied with soap and sanitiser, its toilets clean and supplied, and its drinking water safe. Every quantity is worked out **per member of staff**, so the box follows the headcount.
- **Three sizes:** small office **1–9 staff** (priced at 5 staff), medium office **10–50 staff** (priced at 25), large office **51+ staff** (priced at 75). The same 20 lines are in every size; only the quantities change.
- **What is inside each month:** plasters, sterile gauze, crepe and roller bandages, sterile dressings, first-aid gloves, antiseptic and an instant cold pack (first aid); liquid hand soap, hand sanitiser, paper hand towels, toilet paper, bleach, floor disinfectant, 70% alcohol for desks and phones, and rubber gloves for the cleaner (hygiene); an emergency stock of sanitary pads and liners for the sanitary bin (staff wellbeing); and drinking-water treatment.
- **Built on** Tanzania's OSH (First Aid and Welfare Facilities) Rules 2015: one marked first-aid box and two trained first-aiders per 10–50 staff (rule 4), soap, water or toilet paper and sanitary bins (rule 7), and safe drinking water without shared cups (rule 12). These rules were read **in search results only**; the official text and its First Schedule (the list of box contents) could not be opened.
- **Medicine:** paracetamol for the first-aid cupboard is listed but **not sold or priced by Afya**; the partner pharmacy supplies it (B4).
- **Member price (D4, packing and delivery included):**
  - small office: **TSh 64,000 / 62,000 / 60,000** a month (1 / 6 / 12-month plans; shelf value 66,205);
  - medium office: **TSh 261,500 / 253,500 / 245,500** (shelf 269,756), about **TSh 10,500 per staff member**;
  - large office: **TSh 785,000 / 760,500 / 736,500** (shelf 809,269).
- **Margin check:** medium and large pass on every plan. The **small office fails on the 12-month plan** (−TSh 740 a month), so on that plan it is delivered every two months (one double box, margin +6,015) or collected in store (+2,760).
- **Staff blood-pressure day** (`bp-check`): a nurse screens up to 25 staff on site once a quarter, **TSh 22,000 per visit** charged in the month it happens. This is a **planning estimate** with no Dar quote yet.
- **Taste of luxury:** Premium adds an Air Wick Freshmatic refill for the staff washroom (TSh 16,000 a month, Shoppers). Luxury adds Bath & Body Works foaming hand soap at reception (TSh 28,462, Kenyan price).
- **Old figure not supported.** The old showroom's "Afya Ofisi" price of **TSh 174,500 for 25 staff** has no source. It equals the old item list's goods value at old prices, with nothing left for packing, delivery or the member saving. The sourced medium box for 25 staff is **TSh 261,500** on the 1-month plan (223,500 if the office drops paper towels).
- **Well sourced:** 32 of 70 brand rows were opened on AfyaDepot or Shoppers on 10 Nov 2026. These cover the default brand of every hygiene line, the antiseptic, the cold pack and the sanitary pads: 80–88% of each size's value. Bin liners and water treatment are Kenyan prices.
- **Not well sourced:** every first-aid consumable (plasters, gauze, bandages, dressings, gloves) is a Kenyan price (36 rows, KES × 20.33, not re-opened), because neither Tanzanian site lists them. The box stays **not final** until the First Schedule is read and a Tanzanian wholesaler quotes these items.

## 1. Who it is for, and the guidance it is built from

**Who.** Offices in Dar es Salaam of any size: company and NGO offices, bank branches, clinics' back offices, schools' staff rooms and co-working floors. The buyer is the office manager or HR. Offices rarely injure people; they lose working days to colds, flu and stomach upsets passed across shared desks, phones, kitchens and toilets, and to running out of basic supplies. The box therefore gives hand hygiene, toilets and surfaces as much weight as bandages. It also carries the welfare items the OSH Rules expect, so the office is ready for an inspection.

**Sizes.** The OSH Rules set their first-aid ratio at 10–50 employees. The three sizes follow the old showroom's Kazini bands: Ndogo 1–9, Kati 10–50 and Kubwa 51+.

| Size | Key | Staff | Priced at | First-aid boxes kept stocked | Cleaners / women's toilets (planning) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small office | `s` | 1–9 | 5 | 1 | 1 / 1 |
| Medium office | `m` | 10–50 | 25 | 1 (rule 4: 1 per 10–50) | 1 / 1 |
| Large office | `l` | 51+ | 75 | 2 (1 per 50 staff, extending the rule-4 ratio; planning) | 3 / 3 |

## 2. What changed from the old code

The old showroom had two office boxes:
- `part3c.js` `D.PKG.office` "Afya Ofisi": one "block of 25 staff", 15 lines;
- `D.PKG.kz` stage `ofisi` "Kazini Ofisi Ndogo": about 4 staff, 16 lines;
- plus a `model.js` Kazini model (`A.kazini`, core kit + sector kits);
- plus the front-door price `KZOFF` in `part0.js:1405`.

- **Structure.** The three size bands from `part0.js` (Ndogo 1–9 · Kati 10–50 · Kubwa 51+) became the three stages `s/m/l`. The old "one block per 25 staff" logic is kept inside the formulas (cleaners, women's toilets and BP visits are counted per 25 staff).
- **The small-business sector kits** (`duka`, `chakula`, `saluni`, `karakana`) moved to Afya Biashara (D-3) and are not in this box.
- **Quantities** are now per-staff formulas, not fixed amounts per block. Sanitiser is now 130 ml per staff (old: 1.5 L for 25, i.e. 60 ml). The old 1.5 L gave about 2 rubs a week per person.
- **Removed:**
  - the ergonomic wrist and back supports (Kedley, TSh 25,740–96,160 KE). The old code itself cited Cochrane CD008570: devices alone have limited evidence;
  - surgical masks (old Ofisi Ndogo sector kit: no routine office need);
  - Betadine (a povidone-iodine antiseptic: partner pharmacy, R1);
  - the foil emergency blanket, triangular bandages, scissors and record book as monthly lines. They are durable and moved to the one-off starter kit (3.4).
- **Added:**
  - sterile dressing pads and an instant cold pack in the monthly refill;
  - paper hand towels (WHO handwashing steps end with a single-use towel);
  - paracetamol, listed as a partner-pharmacy medicine. The old code left painkillers "to the business's judgement".
- **Prices.**
  - Every Kenyan row is recomputed from its KES original at 20.33 (the code used × 20).
  - Shoppers prices for 23 rows were re-opened on 10 Nov 2026. AfyaDepot rows (methylated spirit, Emina disinfectant, Firstar cold pack, Panadol, Human Cherish pads) come from each page's schema.org data, opened 10 Nov.
  - The old superstore.co.tz rows (Lit 5 L at 8,250, Jik at 5,850, Rexa roll at 550, Velvex 10-pack) were replaced by Shoppers listings. Only Biox soap and Softcare pads remain from superstore, as swaps.
- **Afya Choice** is new: one value brand per item, and it is the default used for the headline price. The rule is the lowest cost per unit among rows with a real listing, preferring a Tanzanian listing opened on 10 Nov. Biox soap and Softcare pads are cheaper but come from a host that could not be reached, so they are listed as swaps.
- **KZOFF** (TSh 174,500 / 169,500 / 164,500 for 25 staff) is not used. See section 6.

## 3. Stage-by-stage items

Each row lists the brand choices. **Bold** = default brand used for the headline price; ★ = Afya Choice (value brand). Monthly cost = price × units ÷ pack units. Status: TZ = Tanzanian listing; KE = Kenyan listing converted at KES 20.33 (BoT mean, 10 Nov 2026); EST = labelled estimate. Medicines are listed but not priced in the box (B4).

How slow items work: lines used more slowly than one pack a month (antiseptic, bandages, cold pack, alcohol, floor disinfectant in a small office) are charged for one month's use. A new pack ships when the last runs low, as in Mama na Mtoto. Working month = 22 days. Every quantity is a **planning assumption** unless a formula says otherwise; none comes from a published office-consumption study.

## 6. Price check (D4)

- **Formula.** Shelf value = Σ default-brand price × units ÷ pack units, over goods. Medicines and the luxury line are excluded.
- **Member price.** Shelf × (1 − 3% / 6% / 9%), rounded to the nearest 500 and never above shelf.
- **Packing and delivery** are inside the price. Delivery is 3,500. Packing at landed cost:
  - small: one M box (5,000);
  - medium: two L cartons (13,000);
  - large: five L cartons (32,500).

  Carton counts are a planning assumption (about one L carton per 15 staff, driven by toilet paper and soap volume). Confirm them by packing a test box.
- **Margin check** = price − goods at 78% landed cost (R3 planning share, not a supplier quote) − packing − delivery − 1% points.
