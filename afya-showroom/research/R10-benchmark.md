# R10 — Benchmark study: investor documents of breakthrough companies (feeds 0.1, 0.2, 6.1–6.3, 7.5)

Checked 10 Nov 2026. Draft for the founder; the showroom and the brief are unchanged.

**Access note.** None of the seven documents could be opened from this session. `www.sec.gov`, `aboutamazon.com`, every pitch-deck archive and every press site tried were refused by the network proxy (curl and WebFetch alike). Every finding below is therefore **search snippet, page not opened**: it comes from search-result extracts of the filing itself or of press and analyst write-ups, and the source of each is named. Where the snippets said nothing about a question, the cell says **not found**. Nothing is filled in from memory. Before the R10 checklist is used for Phase 7 sign-off, the founder (or a session with `www.sec.gov` allowed) should open the four SEC filings and confirm the quoted lines.

This study is about **document craft only** (B6). It does not take any company's business model as a basis for Afya, and the showroom will not name these companies.

## In plain language

- The best investor documents **open with one plain line and one concrete fact**, and put the facts before the story. When Warby Parker put its founders' letter ahead of the prospectus summary, the SEC staff asked it to move the letter, and it did.
- They **define every key number where it is shown**. Airbnb said its bookings figure is net of cancellations; Chewy said exactly which sales count as "Autoship".
- They **show what one unit earns**: one restaurant, one cohort of customers, one store. Sweetgreen gave targets for a new restaurant's sales, margin, investment and second-year return.
- Their **weak spots are also the lessons**:
  - analysts criticised Sweetgreen's margin for leaving out real costs;
  - Chewy for showing its best customer year;
  - Airbnb for a combined figure that hid more than it showed;
  - Airbnb's 2008 deck for a revenue line built as market × fee.
- **Honest risk is specific and has a number.** Airbnb's letter says plainly that its business fell by nearly 80% and the IPO was put on hold. Jumia listed failed deliveries, returns and voucher abuse, not just "political risk". Sweetgreen named remote work and its four home cities.
- **Afya adopts:** one-line opening, facts before the letter, definitions beside numbers, one-box and one-store economics with the full-cost view, specific numbered risks, and one standing principle repeated in every section.
- **Afya rejects:**
  - combined or flattering metrics;
  - best-case-only charts;
  - "adjusted" margins without the full version;
  - top-down revenue from market × price;
  - boilerplate risk lists;
  - any comparison to another brand as the model.
- **Output:** a 15-test checklist (section 4) for 0.1, 0.2, 6.1–6.3 and 7.5.

## 3. What Afya adopts and what it rejects

### 3a. Adopts

| # | Practice | Seen in | How Afya uses it | Section |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **One plain line, then one concrete fact**, before any story | Airbnb S-1 (four million hosts) | 0.1 opens with what Afya is in one sentence and one sourced number (e.g. the Mama na Mtoto box price against its shelf value) | 0.1, 0.2 |
| A2 | **Facts first, letter second** | Warby Parker (SEC asked for the letter to follow the summary) | The founders' voice sits after the five key numbers and the ask, not before | 0.1 |
| A3 | **A definition beside every key number**, including what it leaves out | Airbnb (GBV net of cancellations); Chewy (Autoship sales defined) | "Member", "founding deposit", "box margin", "break-even month" each get a one-line definition where they first appear | 0.1, 6.1, 6.2 |
| A4 | **Economics of one unit**, with targets kept separate from results | Sweetgreen (new-restaurant targets); Warby Parker (four-wall margin, payback) | One box from price down to margin (6.2); one store month from sales down to profit (6.1); each figure tagged result, model or target | 6.1, 6.2 |
| A5 | **The full-cost view next to any adjusted view** | Lesson from the critique of Sweetgreen's restaurant-level profit | Box margin shown with packing, delivery and points included (D4 already does this); store profit shown after rent, staff and head-office share | 6.1, 6.2 |
| A6 | **A "do customers stay?" exhibit with a fixed definition** | Chewy cohort chart | An empty, defined cohort table (members by joining month, still paying after 1, 3, 6 months), filled only from pre-sale and store data. No retention rate is claimed before then (R3) | 6.1, 7.5 |
| A7 | **Bad news in the founders' own words, with the number** | Airbnb letter ("declined by nearly 80%") | The year-1 loss and the flagship downside (cash to about −TSh 46M, D-20) are stated plainly in 6.3 with the three answers | 6.3, 0.2 |
| A8 | **Specific, on-the-ground risks first** | Jumia (failed deliveries, returns, voucher abuse); Sweetgreen (remote work, four cities) | 6.3 and 7.3 lead with Afya's own: fewer than 300 deposits, churn above 8%, landed cost above about 80% (R6), payment collection (R5), kiosk failure (R2) | 6.3, 7.5 |
| A9 | **A market funnel where every step has a source** | Airbnb deck (2B → 560M → 84M) | 1.3 and its 0.2 slide show each narrowing step with its source; the last step is a member count, not a revenue figure | 0.2 |
| A10 | **One standing principle, repeated word for word** | Amazon 1997 letter (reprinted every year) | "Members never pay more than the shelf price, and every cost is recovered in the price" appears in the same words in 0.1, 6.2 and the 7.5 Q&A | 0.1, 6.2, 7.5 |
| A11 | **Outside proof of demand when there are no sales yet** | Airbnb deck (Couchsurfing users, Craigslist listings) | The founding-member deposit count is Afya's own proof; until it exists, only cited market evidence (D-7) is shown | 0.1, 6.3 |

### 3b. Rejects

| # | Practice | Seen in | Why Afya rejects it |
|---|---|---|---|
| X1 | **A combined metric that hides a weak part** | Airbnb ("gross nights and experiences booked", per Skift) | Deposits and paying members are never added together, and box and walk-in revenue are shown separately |
| X2 | **Showing only the best cohort or case** | Chewy (2015 cohort, per analyst) | The base case is shown by default and the downside is named. Under B5 only one funding option is visible at a time, but every scenario is reachable |
| X3 | **An adjusted margin without the full version** | Sweetgreen (per TechCrunch) | It conflicts with B3 (honest about gaps); see A5 |
| X4 | **Revenue = market × average price** | Airbnb deck (84M trips × $25, per analysis) | The model builds revenue from visitors, joins and churn; no top-down revenue appears in 0.2 |
| X5 | **Boilerplate risk lists without ranking** | Jumia (about 23 factors, many generic) | 7.3 ranks by likelihood × impact; generic country risks are grouped at the end in one line each |
| X6 | **A long letter before the facts** | Warby Parker draft | See A2 |
| X7 | **Any other company as the basis of the idea** | — | B6: these documents teach form only. No section names them or says "the X of Tanzania" |
| X8 | **Targets presented as results** | General risk in every S-1 that mixes the two | Every number is tagged result, model or target (A4) |

## 4. Checklist for 0.1, 0.2, 6.1–6.3 and 7.5

Apply before Phase 7 (Part I). A section passes only if every test that applies to it passes. "Learned from" names the benchmark (section 2) or the rejected practice (section 3b).

| # | Test | Pass condition | Applies to | Learned from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One line, one fact | The first screen states what Afya is in one sentence of 25 words or fewer, followed by one sourced number | 0.1, 0.2 (slide 1) | Airbnb S-1 (A1) |
| 2 | The ask in the first screen | The seed amount for the selected option, what it buys in one line, and "released in three tranches against milestones" appear before the first scroll on desktop | 0.1, 6.3 | A2 |
| 3 | Facts before the letter | No founder narrative appears above the five key numbers and the ask | 0.1, 0.2 | Warby Parker / SEC (A2, X6) |
| 4 | Defined beside the number | Every headline figure has a one-line definition within the same card or table, including what it excludes; for example, member counts exclude unconverted deposits | 0.1, 6.1, 6.2, 6.3 | Airbnb GBV, Chewy Autoship (A3) |
| 5 | Result, model or target | Every number carries one of three tags (result, model, target); no target is written in the past tense | 0.1, 0.2, 6.1, 6.3 | A4, X8 |
| 6 | One unit, top to bottom | 6.2 walks one named box from price to margin, and 6.1 one store month from sales to profit. Each line is sourced or marked estimate, and the lines add up to the shilling | 6.1, 6.2 | Sweetgreen, Warby Parker (A4) |
| 7 | Full cost beside adjusted | Any margin that leaves out a cost (packing, delivery, points, rent, head office) is shown next to the full-cost figure | 6.1, 6.2 | Sweetgreen critique (A5, X3) |
| 8 | Nothing combined to flatter | No figure adds deposits to members, store to online, or two categories that are weak on their own | 0.1, 6.1, 6.3 | Airbnb critique (X1) |
| 9 | Not the best case only | The base case is the default; the downside case and its lowest cash point are stated in the same section; the gate's fallback is shown | 6.1, 6.3, 0.2 | Chewy critique (X2) |
| 10 | Bad news, in plain words | The year-1 loss, the break-even month and the downside cash figure appear as sentences in the founders' voice, with the three answers (D-20) | 6.3, 0.2 | Airbnb letter (A7) |
| 11 | Specific risks first | The top five risks shown are Afya-specific, each with a trigger number and an answer. Generic risks are grouped after them | 6.3, 0.2 (risk slide), 7.5 Q&A | Jumia, Sweetgreen (A8, X5) |
| 12 | Money adds up | Use of funds sums to the seed to the shilling; tranche amounts = seed × split; each milestone names its evidence document; all read from `model.js` | 6.3, 0.1 | Part I; R4 §6 |
| 13 | Funnel, not multiplication | Any market figure is a sourced funnel ending in members; no revenue figure is market × price | 0.2 | Airbnb deck (A9, X4) |
| 14 | One principle, same words | The customer-funded rule (member price never above shelf; every cost recovered in the price) appears in identical wording in 0.1, 6.2 and 7.5, in both languages | 0.1, 6.2, 7.5 | Amazon 1997 (A10) |
| 15 | Every figure traceable | Every figure in these sections appears in the 7.5 source list with a working link and check date; snippet-only or unopened sources are marked as such; nothing names another brand as the model (B6) | 7.5 (and all listed sections) | Part B1, X7 |
