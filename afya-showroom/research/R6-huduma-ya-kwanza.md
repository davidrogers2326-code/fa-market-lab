Feeds section 3.5. Prices checked 10 Nov 2026. Data file: `data/boxes/huduma-ya-kwanza.json` (validated against `data/boxes/SCHEMA.md`). Built from the earlier code box `D.PKG.aid` in `part3c.js`. The showroom code and the brief are unchanged.

## In plain language

- Huduma ya Kwanza is a home first-aid kit for every household, especially one with young children or older people. The household buys the **complete kit** once; **refills** come every 3 months.
- It is built on WHO guidance that could be opened: the burns fact sheet (cool running water about 20 minutes, a clean non-stick cover, nothing else), the diarrhoea fact sheet (ORS and zinc), the child-injury report (safe storage) and the tetanus fact sheet. **Red Cross and IFRC guidance could not be opened from this session, so neither is cited.**
- Kit: a lidded box, a bilingual card, thermometer, scissors, tweezers, plasters, gauze, non-stick pads, roller, crepe and triangular bandages, tape, an instant cold pack, antiseptic, calamine, gloves, spirit for tools and pocket hand rub.
- ORS, zinc and adult and children’s paracetamol are listed as medicines: the partner pharmacy supplies them, not priced in the box (B4).
- **Member price:** complete kit TSh 77,000 (1-month plan) to 72,000 (12-month), shelf 79,330. Refill every 3 months: 18,500 to 17,500. **A first year: 132,500 to 124,500.** For comparison only, AfyaDepot sells a ready-filled home kit at TSh 45,000 with contents not itemised.
- **Fix applied:** a refill (about 19,000) cannot pay its own packing and delivery (−2,044 on 12 months if sent alone). Refills therefore **ride in another Afya box** the household already receives, or are **collected at the free 3-monthly kit check** in store. The kit itself pays its way delivered in an S box.
- **Taste of luxury:** Premium, a second ready-filled kit for the car or handbag (TSh 45,000, TZ); Luxury, a metal wall cabinet (TSh 99,000, TZ).
- **Sourcing is weak:** only 13% of the kit’s default value is Tanzanian. AfyaDepot and Shoppers list no plasters, gauze, bandages, gloves, scissors or tweezers, so those are Kenyan prices from the code (24 TZ, 39 KE, 1 EST of 64 rows). Every core item has a sourced price or, for the Afya-printed card, a labelled estimate, so `final` is true.

## 1. Who it is for, and the guidance it is built from

**English.** For every household, and especially one with young children or older people. Injuries at home are common and mostly minor (cuts, grazes, burns from cooking and hot drinks, sprains, insect bites), but the first minutes decide how they heal. A child with diarrhoea, still the third leading cause of death in children aged 1–59 months (WHO), needs rehydration to start at home.

The kit is built on what WHO says to do first. Burns: cool with running water for about 20 minutes, remove rings and clothing not stuck to the wound, cover with a clean non-adherent dressing or cloth, and never apply ice, oil, butter, toothpaste or traditional pastes (WHO burns fact sheet). The kit therefore carries non-stick pads and no burn gel. Diarrhoea: ORS and a 10–14 day course of zinc (WHO), supplied by the partner pharmacy. Poisoning: medicines are stored in one closed box out of children’s reach (WHO World report on child injury prevention). Wounds: clean, cover and check tetanus protection after any dirty or deep wound (WHO tetanus fact sheet). Red Cross and IFRC first-aid guidance could not be consulted for this box, so it is not cited.

The household buys the complete kit once. Refills come every three months to replace what was used and anything near expiry; they ride in another Afya box the household already receives, or are collected at the free three-monthly kit check in store.

**Kiswahili.** Kwa kila kaya, hasa yenye watoto wadogo au wazee. Majeraha nyumbani ni ya kawaida na mengi ni madogo (vidonda, michubuko, kuungua wakati wa kupika au kwa vinywaji vya moto, kuteguka, kuumwa na wadudu), lakini dakika za kwanza huamua yanavyopona. Mtoto anayeharisha, ambalo bado ni chanzo cha tatu cha vifo vya watoto wenye miezi 1–59 (WHO), anahitaji kuanza kurejeshewa maji akiwa nyumbani.

Sanduku limejengwa juu ya hatua za kwanza zinazoshauriwa na WHO. Kuungua: poza kwa maji ya bomba kwa takriban dakika 20, ondoa pete na nguo zisizoshikamana na jeraha, funika kwa bandeji safi isiyonata au kitambaa safi, na kamwe usipake barafu, mafuta, siagi, dawa ya meno wala dawa za kienyeji (taarifa ya WHO kuhusu kuungua). Kwa hiyo sanduku lina pedi zisizonata na halina jeli ya kuungua. Kuharisha: ORS na zinki kwa siku 10–14 (WHO), zinazotolewa na famasia mshirika. Sumu: dawa huhifadhiwa ndani ya sanduku moja lililofungwa, mbali na watoto (ripoti ya WHO kuhusu kuzuia majeraha kwa watoto). Majeraha: safisha, funika na hakikisha kinga ya pepopunda baada ya jeraha lolote chafu au lenye kina (taarifa ya WHO kuhusu pepopunda). Miongozo ya huduma ya kwanza ya Msalaba Mwekundu na IFRC haikuweza kupatikana kwa sanduku hili, hivyo haijatajwa.

Kaya hununua sanduku kamili mara moja. Vitu vya kujaza huja kila miezi mitatu kuchukua nafasi ya vilivyotumika na vinavyokaribia kuisha muda; huja ndani ya sanduku lingine la Afya ambalo kaya tayari inapokea, au huchukuliwa dukani wakati wa ukaguzi wa bure wa sanduku kila miezi mitatu.

### Clinic line

The brief’s B16 clinic line applies to disease-care boxes; this kit carries a “when to go to the clinic now” line in the same place.

- **EN:** This kit is for minor injuries and for the first minutes before help arrives. Any medicine, and its dose for a child, is set by the pharmacist or your clinic. Go to a clinic or call for help straight away for a burn larger than the person’s palm or on the face, hands, feet or genitals, an electrical or chemical burn, bleeding that does not stop with firm pressure, a deep or dirty wound, a suspected broken bone, a head injury, a snake bite, a fever in a baby under three months, or a child with diarrhoea who cannot drink or is very sleepy.
- **SW:** Sanduku hili ni kwa majeraha madogo na kwa dakika za kwanza kabla msaada haujafika. Dawa yoyote, na dozi yake kwa mtoto, huwekwa na mfamasia au kliniki yako. Nenda kliniki au omba msaada mara moja kwa kuungua kukubwa kuliko kiganja cha mtu au usoni, mikononi, miguuni au sehemu za siri, kuungua kwa umeme au kemikali, damu isiyokoma kwa kubana kwa nguvu, jeraha lenye kina au chafu, mfupa unaodhaniwa kuvunjika, jeraha la kichwa, kuumwa na nyoka, homa kwa mtoto chini ya miezi mitatu, au mtoto anayeharisha asiyeweza kunywa au mwenye usingizi mzito.
