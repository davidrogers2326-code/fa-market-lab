# R6 — Afya Biashara (small-business package, five trade sizes)

Feeds section 3.5. Prices checked 10 Nov 2026. Data file: `data/boxes/afya-biashara.json` (validated against `data/boxes/SCHEMA.md`; `final: true`). Draft for the founder; the showroom code and the brief are unchanged.

## In plain language

- Afya Biashara is one monthly package for small businesses of about four staff. Founder decision D-3 turns the five old Afya Kazini trade kits into its five sizes, modelled as stages: **shop, restaurant, salon, workshop, small office**.
- Every size carries the same core: a refilled first-aid box, soap, sanitiser, disinfectant, bleach, toilet paper, paper towels, sanitary pads and bin liners for the staff toilet, and drinking-water treatment. These follow the welfare and first-aid duties the old code cites from Tanzania's OSH Rules 2015 (the Rules themselves could not be opened here).
- Each trade adds what its own risks need: food-handling gloves, hair nets and burn dressings for restaurants; gloves, 70% alcohol, fresh blades and a sharps box for salons; ear plugs, cut-resistant gloves, eye wash and burn dressings for workshops.
- 12-month plan, per month: **Shop or kiosk 47,500**, **Restaurant or food stall 118,000**, **Salon or barbershop 102,000**, **Workshop or building site 124,000**, **Small office 50,500**.
- Included partner services (first-aid training seat, extinguisher service, and pest control for restaurants) are billed in the month they happen: 53,000 a year for most trades, 158,000 for a restaurant.
- **Every size pays its way on every plan.** The shop and small-office sizes are thin on 12 months (+975 and +1,410).
- Every core item has a sourced price, so the box is **final**. Many first-aid and workshop items are Kenyan prices (labelled KE).
- R8 asks what these businesses consume and pay now: see `research/R8-business-hotels.md`.

## 1. Who it is for, and the guidance it is built from

**English.** For a small business of about four staff: a shop or kiosk, a restaurant or food stall, a salon or barbershop, a workshop or building site, or a small office. Founder decision D-3 makes the five old Afya Kazini trade kits the sizes of this one business package.



Tanzania’s Occupational Safety and Health (First Aid and Welfare Facilities) Rules 2015 bind every employer. As cited in the old code (the Rules could not be opened here), they require a stocked, marked first-aid box with a treatment record, soap and toilet paper for employees, a clean toilet with a sanitary bin, and safe drinking water. A search result quoting the Rules adds one box and two trained first-aiders for 10–50 employees. Each trade then gets the protection its own risks call for, from WHO’s Five Keys to Safer Food in a kitchen to fresh blades and gloves in a salon and eye and hearing protection in a workshop.



One delivery a month keeps every item in stock, the first-aid box is checked at each delivery, and the business gets one invoice instead of many shop runs.

### Safety line (shown on the page)

- **EN:** First aid is for small injuries only. A deep cut, a burn larger than the palm, something in the eye that does not wash out, or any injury with heavy bleeding goes to a health facility straight away. Afya does not sell medicines: the licensed partner pharmacy supplies them.
- **SW:** Huduma ya kwanza ni kwa majeraha madogo tu. Jeraha kubwa, jeraha la moto kubwa kuliko kiganja, kitu jichoni kisichotoka kwa kuosha, au jeraha lolote linalotoa damu nyingi linapelekwa kituo cha afya mara moja. Afya haiuzi dawa: famasia mshirika yenye leseni ndiyo inayozitoa.
