## 1. Who it is for, and the guidance it is built from

**English.**

Binti is the box for a teenage girl, from about 10 years old, around her first period, to 18. It brings what puberty asks for, so she is never caught at school without a pad: day and night pads for two cycles, sealable bags for used pads, an antiperspirant, a gentle face wash, soap, toothpaste and a toothbrush, body lotion, a pocket hand sanitiser and a mosquito repellent. The first box adds a hot-water bottle for cramps, a cycle diary with a printed guide, and a treated net.

**Why.** WHO asks that menstruation be treated as a health issue, not only a hygiene one. In a 2024 study of 486 schoolgirls in Mwanza, 16% had missed school at their last period and three in four had period pain (figures from the code; the article could not be re-opened). The period items are kept modest: the amounts cover a typical cycle for a girl, and Afya Siku is the separate, full period box for women.

**Iron.** Iron-deficiency anaemia is among the leading causes of ill health in adolescents (WHO). Where 20% or more of women are anaemic, WHO recommends intermittent iron and folic acid for menstruating women and adolescent girls; Tanzania’s 2022 survey found 37% of women anaemic (fact sheet seen in a search result only). The weekly tablet is a medicine: it is supplied by the partner pharmacy on its advice and is not in the box price, like ibuprofen for cramps and benzoyl peroxide gel for acne.

**HPV.** WHO makes HPV vaccination a priority for girls aged 9 to 14. Tanzania gives one free dose to girls at 9 through the public programme (UNICEF, search result); the box reminds families who missed it.

**When to see a clinic.** Bleeding for more than seven days, soaking a pad every hour, pain that stops her going to school even with tablets, no period by 15, or acne that scars.

**Kiswahili.**

Binti ni sanduku la msichana balehe, kuanzia takriban miaka 10, karibu na hedhi yake ya kwanza, hadi miaka 18. Linaleta kile ambacho balehe inahitaji, ili asikutwe shuleni bila pedi: pedi za mchana na za usiku za mizunguko miwili, mifuko inayofungika kwa pedi zilizotumika, roll-on ya kuzuia jasho, sabuni laini ya uso, sabuni, dawa ya meno na mswaki, losheni ya mwili, kitakasa mikono cha mfukoni na dawa ya kufukuza mbu. **Kwa nini.** WHO inataka hedhi ichukuliwe kuwa suala la afya, si la usafi tu. Katika utafiti wa mwaka 2024 wa wasichana 486 wa shule Mwanza, asilimia 16 walikosa shule wakati wa hedhi yao iliyopita na watatu kati ya wanne walikuwa na maumivu ya hedhi (takwimu kutoka kwenye msimbo; makala haikuweza kufunguliwa upya). Bidhaa za hedhi ni za kiasi: kiasi kinatosha mzunguko wa kawaida wa msichana, na Afya Siku ndilo sanduku kamili tofauti la hedhi kwa wanawake.

**Madini ya chuma.** Upungufu wa damu unaotokana na ukosefu wa madini ya chuma ni miongoni mwa sababu kuu za afya duni kwa vijana (WHO). Pale asilimia 20 au zaidi ya wanawake wana upungufu wa damu, WHO inapendekeza madini ya chuma na foliki kwa vipindi kwa wanawake na wasichana balehe wanaopata hedhi; utafiti wa Tanzania wa 2022 ulionyesha asilimia 37 ya wanawake wana upungufu wa damu (karatasi iliyoonekana kwenye matokeo ya utafutaji tu). Kidonge cha kila wiki ni dawa: hutolewa na famasia mshirika kwa ushauri wake na hakimo kwenye bei ya sanduku, kama ilivyo ibuprofen kwa maumivu na jeli ya benzoyl peroxide kwa chunusi.

**HPV.** WHO inaweka chanjo ya HPV kuwa kipaumbele kwa wasichana wa miaka 9 hadi 14. Tanzania hutoa dozi moja bure kwa wasichana wa miaka 9 kupitia mpango wa serikali (UNICEF, matokeo ya utafutaji); sanduku huzikumbusha familia zilizoikosa.

**Wakati wa kwenda kliniki.** Damu zaidi ya siku saba, pedi kujaa kila saa, maumivu yanayomzuia kwenda shule hata akitumia vidonge, kutopata hedhi hadi miaka 15, au chunusi zinazoacha makovu.

## 7. Does it pay its way?

**Check (D4):** price − goods at landed cost − packing − delivery (3,500) − 1% points ≥ 0. Landed cost uses R3's planning shares (brand 78%, pharm 72%, own 50%).

**Monthly, as in the code (S box, packing 3,500 + delivery 3,500):**

| Box | Monthly shelf | 1-month | 6-month | 12-month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Every two months after that | 27,720 | -1,774 | -2,764 | -3,754 |

Plainly: **no.** Monthly delivery loses money on every plan.

**Fix applied: every two months.** Margin per box:

| Box | Size | Delivered on its own: 1 / 6 / 12-month | Riding inside another Afya box (no delivery; S inner pack): 1 / 6 / 12-month |
|---|---|---|---|
| First box · first periods | M | 10,472 / 7,997 / 6,017 | 15,472 / 12,997 / 11,017 |
| Every two months after that | S | 3,452 / 1,472 / **-13** | 6,952 / 4,972 / 3,487 |

**Result.** One box every two months, packed with two months of goods. Delivered on its own it pays its way on every plan except: Every two months after that on the 12-month plan. Those boxes ride inside another Afya box delivered to the same household (one delivery, no extra delivery cost) or are collected in store. Monthly delivery is not offered: a one-month basket cannot carry packing and delivery.

Over a year of boxes delivered on their own (first box once, then the boxes a year shown in section 6), the margin is 31,184 (1-month), 16,829 (6-month), 5,939 (12-month); with the failing boxes riding, every box is positive. Margins stay thin (1–7% of price on recurring boxes); they turn negative if goods land above about 80–82% of shelf instead of 78%.
